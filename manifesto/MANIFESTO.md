# Just Prompt, Motherfucker

*Do you speak it?*

We are two programmers who accepted the new way of working without liking it. We are not
full-time builders anymore. We rent a model by the token, we work someone else's land, and we
ship what the landlord's tools allow. Call it technofeudalism, call it what you want. We took
the deal.

What we did not sign up for is the hype stacked on top of it.

We are tired of *harness engineering*, *agent orchestration*, *spec-driven development*,
*context engineering*, *multi-agent swarms*, *loops*, *forty skills*, *twenty MCP servers*,
*prompt engineering* and anything else getting in the way of... **Just Prompt, Motherfucker**.

## What we believe

- **Harness is tooling.** Claude Code, Codex, whatever comes next. Let the people who build
  harnesses build harnesses. We build the business.
- **Prompting is communication.** Your language, your typos, your swearing. Whoever can explain a
  problem to a colleague can prompt. Whoever can't, no template will save.
- **Prompting is software engineering as always.** Tests, linters, types, small commits, green CI.
  The gates did not move. Only the typist did.
- **Prompting is scientific exploration.** Hypothesis, experiment, evidence. Reproduce before
  fixing. Screenshot or it didn't happen.
- **Talk before you build.** "Let's just talk for now, don't touch anything." Most of the value
  happens before the first line of code.
- **Ask. And let it ask you back.** A question is a prompt. An agent that asks the next right
  question, one at a time, walks you to the design faster than one that dumps a plan. Don't quiz
  me, guide me. A dialogue, not a questionnaire.
- **Root cause, not laps.** When it starts going in circles, stop and ask what the actual problem
  is. Then fix that, for good.
- **The more markdown, the more slop.** One document you read and vetted is a prompt. A folder of
  documents nobody reads is a landfill.
- **The compiler is the gate.** Types, tests, lint. That is what checks the model. Everything else
  is optional and probably a conference talk.
- **Less is more.** Delete before you add. A prompt with fewer words beats a system with more
  layers.
